Cloudflare Registrar offers several advantages over other registrars, such as domain name registration renewal without markups fees. You only pay what is charged by registries and ICANN. Cloudflare Registrar also offers additional security features, such as free, one-click activation for Domain Name System Security Extensions (DNSSEC), custom domain protection, and WHOIS redaction.

You can buy your domain from Cloudflare Registrar or from a third party. Even when you buy it from a third party, you can manage your domain with Cloudflare to benefit from all the features Cloudflare offers.
